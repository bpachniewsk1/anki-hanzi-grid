# HSK 3.1 character lists

`level1.txt` … `level7-9.txt` list the characters of each level of the 2025
HSK syllabus (新版HSK考试大纲, released December 2025), one character per
line. "HSK 3.1" is an unofficial name for that 2025 version, used to tell it
apart from the 2021 HSK 3.0 syllabus.

Copied unchanged from the `sources/HSK3.1` folder of
"More than enough Hanzi" (MteH) by Rebecca J. Stones:
https://github.com/becky82/mteh/tree/main/sources/HSK3.1
