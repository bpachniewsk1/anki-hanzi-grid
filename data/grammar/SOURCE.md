# Grammar data

Two files make the Grammar view.

## index.json

The list of grammar points by level: for each, the id and title of its
article on the **Chinese Grammar Wiki** by AllSet Learning,
https://resources.allsetlearning.com/chinese/grammar/ . The wiki's text is
licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/);
only the article titles and ids are copied here, as an index with links.
Each point links to its article, which is the full, verified explanation.

The wiki groups points by CEFR band. Hanzi Grid labels them with the HSK
levels the wiki aligns them to: A1 → HSK 1, A2 → HSK 2, B1 → HSK 3,
B2 → HSK 4–5, C1 → HSK 6–9. The new HSK 3.0 grammar syllabus (2021) is not
freely licensed, so the exact level of a point there may differ.

## points.json

Short notes for about 120 of those points: the pattern, an explanation and
example sentences with pinyin and English, written for Hanzi Grid (not
copied from the wiki) and licensed like the add-on, AGPL-3.0-or-later.
The rule each note describes is the one in the linked wiki article; read
the article for the full picture and more examples. Corrections welcome at
https://github.com/bpachniewsk1/anki-hanzi-grid/issues .
