See every Chinese character you're learning on one screen, grouped by HSK level and coloured by how well you know it.

Quick start

1. In Anki's main window, open Tools → Hanzi Grid. 2. Click ⚙ Settings, tick your Chinese deck(s), pick the word, pinyin and meaning fields, and click Save.

Using the Anki-xiehanzi "New HSK (2025)" deck? The defaults already fit it, so you can skip step 2. Features
  • One tile per character, coloured by your best card containing it: not studied, learning, young, mature, well known (a 45+ day interval and 2 correct reviews in a row, deep teal with a gold line) and mastered (a 3+ month interval and 3 correct reviews in a row). Only your answers count: Set due date doesn't change the colour, and suspended cards are ignored. 学 turns green once you know 学习 or 学生.
  • Five views. Characters, grouped by the official HSK 3.1 character list (2025 HSK syllabus) or by your deck's word levels; Words, one tile per word grouped by the words' HSK levels, which is what the exam counts; Measure words: words grouped by the classifier they take (本 → 书, 课本, 杂志 …), with the measure word's own tile and progress bar; and Grammar: the grammar points of each level (from the Chinese Grammar Wiki, with links), each with a short note and example sentences, a tile showing how well you know each point's key word (过, 着, 的, 得, 地, 把, 被 …), and a checkbox to tick once you've mastered a point, with progress per level and a To do filter; and Reading: paste any Chinese text to see every character coloured by your status, how much of it you've studied, and the words to learn by level.
  • A summary under the deck list: one bar per level, "+N this week" and an Open link (can be turned off).
  • A progress bar for each level and for all levels together, with "+N this week".
  • Hover a tile to see its pinyin and meaning.
  • Click a character to open its page: every word that contains it, by level, each coloured by how well you know it. Click a word there (or in the Words view) to see the answer side of its card. Shift+click to find cards in the Browser.
  • Next up under each level: the first characters or words you haven't studied yet.
  • A red dot marks weak tiles you keep forgetting. A Show filter and a Find box.
  • Five looks, switched instantly from the Look menu in the grid: Modern, Seal (ink blue for well known, a red seal stamp for mastered), Ink (ink shades), Clear (colour-blind safe) and Classic.
  • Hanzi font and size next to Settings: Hei 黑, Song 宋 or Kai 楷, the three styles you meet when reading, and Small, Medium or Large.
  • Settings window: choose decks, level source, fields and the mature, well known, mastered and weak thresholds.
  • Offline and safe: it never changes your cards or notes and sends nothing anywhere. Ticked grammar points are saved in your collection, so they sync.

Card shown: from the Anki-xiehanzi deck. About the levels "HSK 3.1" is an unofficial name for the 2025 version of the HSK 3.0 syllabus. With "From my deck's words", a character's level is the lowest HSK level of any word in your decks that contains it; these are not official character levels, and the grid says so.
