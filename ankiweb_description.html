See every Chinese character you're learning on one screen, grouped by HSK level and coloured by how well you know it. Features
  • One tile per character, coloured by your best card containing it: not studied, learning, young, mature. 学 turns green once you know 学习 or 学生.
  • Grouped by HSK level, using the official HSK 3.1 character list (2025 HSK syllabus), or levels worked out from your deck's words.
  • A progress bar for each level and for all levels together.
  • Hover a character to see its pinyin and meaning.
  • Click a character to see the answer side of the cards of every word that contains it. Shift+click to find them in the Browser.
  • Settings window: choose decks, level source, fields and the mature threshold.
  • Read-only and offline: it never changes your cards and sends nothing anywhere.

Card shown: from the Anki-xiehanzi deck. How to use Tools → Hanzi Grid. Click ⚙ Settings in the grid (or Config in Tools → Add-ons) to pick your decks and fields. The defaults fit the Anki-xiehanzi "New HSK (2025)" deck; for other decks, choose the fields that hold the Chinese word (simplified characters), pinyin and meaning. About the levels "HSK 3.1" is an unofficial name for the 2025 version of the HSK 3.0 syllabus. With "From my deck's words", a character's level is the lowest HSK level of any word in your decks that contains it; these are not official character levels, and the grid says so.
